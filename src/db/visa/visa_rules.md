# Egypt Visa Rules and Entry Requirements

## 1. General notice

- Visa and entry rules depend on nationality, passport type, purpose of visit, and any applicable exception.
- The supplied e-Visa information states that Egyptian authorities may refuse entry even when an e-Visa has been issued.
- Do not treat the summary in this file as a substitute for checking the relevant official source for an individual traveler.

## 2. Egypt e-Visa

The supplied e-Visa FAQ describes the e-Visa as an official document issued to citizens of listed countries. Applicants enter their details and pay by credit or debit card. The e-Visa is sent electronically, and arrival authorities can check it.

### Nationalities listed as e-Visa eligible in the supplied text

Albania; Argentina; Armenia; Australia; Austria; Azerbaijan; Bahrain; Belarus; Belgium; Bolivia; Bosnia and Herzegovina; Brazil; Bulgaria; Canada; Chile; China; Taiwan (listed in the supplied text as China/Taiwan); Colombia; Croatia; Cyprus; Czech Republic; Denmark; Ecuador; Estonia; Finland; France; Georgia; Germany; Greece; Hungary; Iceland; India; Iraq; Ireland; Italy; Japan; South Korea; Kuwait; Latvia; Lithuania; Luxembourg; North Macedonia; Malaysia; Malta; Mexico; Moldova; Monaco; Montenegro; Netherlands; New Zealand; Norway; Oman; Paraguay; Peru; Poland; Portugal; Qatar; Kosovo; Romania; Russian Federation; San Marino; Montenegro; Saudi Arabia; Serbia; Singapore; Slovakia; Slovenia; South Africa; Spain; Sweden; Switzerland; Ukraine; United Arab Emirates; United Kingdom; United States; Uruguay; Vatican City; Venezuela; Vietnam.

## 3. Tourism e-Visa fees

The supplied e-Visa information lists the following tourism visa fees:

- Single-entry tourism e-Visa: **USD 30**
- Multiple-entry tourism e-Visa: **USD 65**

The supplied Ministry of Interior text separately mentions consular fees for some visa categories. No additional consular-fee amount was supplied.

## 4. Entry documents and passport validity

The supplied e-Visa information lists these requirements:

- Passport valid for at least 6 months after arrival.
- Printed copy of the e-Visa.
- Travel itinerary.
- Supporting letter for business or family visits.
- Hotel booking/details for tourists.

The exact applicability of each item to each nationality and visa category was not specified in the supplied summary and needs verification.

## 5. Other visa categories mentioned in the supplied Ministry of Interior text

The supplied text mentions:

- Single-entry visa: USD 30 plus a consular fee.
- Multiple-entry visa valid for 6 months: USD 65 plus a consular fee.
- Five-year multiple-entry visa: USD 700, with a maximum stay of 180 days per trip; described as available through Egyptian diplomatic missions abroad only.
- Transit visa: free for 96 hours under specified conditions.
- Arrival-visa facilitation or other exceptions for some travelers who hold previously used multiple-entry visas from certain countries, qualifying residence permits, or meet nationality-, age-, family-, study-, treatment-, organization-, or tourism-group-related conditions.
- A “Gulf/South Sinai stamp” for certain eligible travelers, allowing visits to Sharm El-Sheikh, Dahab, Nuweiba, Saint Catherine, and Taba for up to 15 days under specified conditions.

The exact conditions and country lists for these categories were not fully reproduced in this summary. They must be captured from the source before rules are used for automated decisions.
