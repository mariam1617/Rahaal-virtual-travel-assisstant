# Transportation in Egypt

## 1. International Airports

Egypt has several international airports that travelers can choose from depending on where they wish to start and end their trip.

The airports listed in the source are:

- Cairo International Airport
- Sphinx International Airport
- Hurghada International Airport
- Marsa Alam International Airport
- Sharm al-Sheikh International Airport
- Taba International Airport
- Luxor International Airport
- Aswan International Airport
- Borg al-Arab International Airport
- Al-Alamein International Airport (opening soon, according to the source)
- Marsa Matruh International Airport
- Asyut International Airport
- Suhag International Airport

## 2. Domestic Airports

The source lists the following domestic airports:

- Port Said Airport
- Al-Kharga Airport
- Abu Simbel Airport

The source mentions EgyptAir, Nile Air, and Air Cairo for domestic flights.

## 3. Sea Ports

Travelers can also enter Egypt through the following sea ports:

- Alexandria Port
- Hurghada Port
- Safaga Port
- Taba Marina Port
- Nuweiba Port
- Ras Ghaleb Port

## 4. Getting Around in Cities

Ride-hailing apps are widely used in Greater Cairo and Alexandria. Taxis are readily available and are required by law to have a working meter. Travelers should make sure the meter is turned on when the ride starts.

## 5. Domestic Air Travel

Domestic flights can provide a short, approximately 60-minute journey to many destinations inside Egypt.

The source mentions EgyptAir, Nile Air, and Air Cairo as airlines that travelers can book with online.

For the airport list, the source refers to:
https://www.experienceegypt.eg/en/usefulinfo#HowtoGetThere

## 6. Railway Transportation

Air-conditioned trains are described as an option for travel between Cairo and:

- Alexandria
- Marsa Matruh
- The Delta
- The Canal Zone
- Luxor
- Aswan

The source describes first-class tickets as offering waiter service, reclining armchairs, and on-board entertainment.

Sleeper cars are described as having a two-bed cabin and a sink, with access to a dining car and a bar serving breakfast and dinner.

The source states that schedules and online tickets are available through the Egyptian Railways website. Seats can be reserved up to seven days in advance, and return tickets may not necessarily be available for purchase at the journey's starting point.

Source:
https://www.experienceegypt.eg/en/usefulinfo

## 7. Bus Transportation

The source identifies three main bus companies operating across Egypt:

### Upper Egypt Bus Company
Connects Cairo to the Nile Valley, Fayoum, the inner oases, and the Red Sea Coast, extending south beyond Marsa Alam.

### East Delta Bus Company
Serves Sinai and the Suez Canal Zone.

### West and Middle Delta Bus Company
Serves the Nile Delta and Alexandria, and extends northwest to Marsa Matruh and Siwa.

The source states that tickets can be booked online.

## 8. Car, Motorcycle, and Bicycle Rental

Renting a car gives travelers flexibility over their schedules. The source also mentions the option of hiring a driver.

Private rental companies are described as offering online booking and vehicle pickup from airports or hotels.

Motorcycles and bicycles are mentioned as options for exploring small beach towns, local sites, and remote beaches.

The source lists the following destinations for two-wheeler exploration:

- Luxor
- Aswan
- Hurghada
- Dahab
- Sharm al-Sheikh
- Al-Gouna
- Siwa

## 9. Boat Transportation and Nile Cruises

The source describes several forms of boat transportation:

- **Nile taxis:** Available in Cairo for travel between neighborhoods.
- **Nile cruises:** Many operate between Luxor and Aswan, with trips lasting three to seven nights and stops at major attractions.
- **Dahabeyas:** Luxury boats operating between Aswan and Luxor.
- **Local ferries:** Available at various points for crossing the Nile and Suez Canal.
- **Feluccas:** Available in Cairo, Luxor, and Aswan for transportation and sightseeing.

The source says that online searches can provide information about Nile taxi rates, booking options, and pickup and drop-off points.

## 10. Travel to Remote and Desert Destinations

For visits to deserts, secluded sites, and less-visited destinations, the source recommends checking with a hotel, licensed guide, authorized travel agency, or tour operator to confirm whether the destination is open to visitors and to arrange the necessary travel.
